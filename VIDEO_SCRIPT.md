# Rahsia: 3-minute pitch video script

**Length:** 3 minutes or less (hard limit from the event). Aim for 2:45 to 2:55.
**Language:** simple English. Speak at a calm pace (about 130 words per minute).
**Use:** record after the product is finished. Render by 14:30 and upload as **public** (YouTube or Vimeo). See TASKS.md.
**Data:** fake data only. Show a small line on screen: "Demo uses fake data."

---

## Before you record (read this first)

1. **Only show what works.** If a scene is not built, cut it and shorten the voiceover. Do not fake it.
2. **Fill the numbers from real results.** Every `[ ]` in scene 8 must come from the measured result. If a target was missed, say the real number. Never use the target as if it were the result.
3. **The word "private".** Say "private match check" only if the match step uses a maintained PSI library. If it only hashes tags, say "match check" and leave out "private".
4. **Claims.** Never say Rahsia stops idea theft. Say it reduces early sharing and records what was shared. The receipt is evidence, not a legal contract.
5. **Network proof.** Scene 5 needs the network monitor to be ready and the internet to be really off.

## Script

| # | Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | Title card "Rahsia". Then a founder at a table, one pitch slide. | "We once pitched our idea to a large company. We never knew what they really wanted: to invest, to partner, or just to hire our team. So founders hold back. Investors hold back too. The first meeting stays shallow." |
| 2 | 0:20–0:35 | One slide: **98 equity deals, Q1 2026**. Small source line: DealStreetAsia, checked 2026-10-27. | "And the market is tight. Southeast Asia had its lowest number of startup deals in at least eight years in the first quarter of 2026. Every early connection that dies is a lost chance." |
| 3 | 0:35–0:50 | Rahsia logo. Two laptops side by side. Internet-off icon. | "This is Rahsia. *Rahsia* means secret in Malay. A startup and an investor check if they fit before they reveal anything. It all runs on each person's own laptop, with Gemma 4. No cloud AI." |
| 4 | 0:50–1:20 | **Screen recording, split.** Left laptop: startup types messy notes (English and Malay mixed). Gemma builds the profile. Right laptop: investor does the same. Show the person reviewing the profile. | "The startup types messy notes, in English, in Malay, or both. Gemma 4 turns them into a clean profile. The code checks the profile against a schema, and the person reviews it before it is used. The investor does the same on a second laptop. The internet is off. The laptops talk over a local link only." |
| 5 | 1:20–1:50 | **No-match case.** Both screens show "No match". Show "0 fields shared". Show the network monitor with almost no traffic. | "First case: they do not fit. Both screens say no match. The other side learned zero fields. A normal online form would have shown everything. Here is the network monitor: almost nothing was sent." |
| 6 | 1:50–2:25 | **Match case.** Both click "Share overlap". Chips appear: sector, stage, money range, place. Then the draft intro and slide outline appear. | "Second case: they fit. Both sides agree to open up. They see only the overlap: sector, stage, money range and place. Then Gemma 4 drafts an intro message and a slide outline. It uses only what both sides agreed to share." |
| 7 | 2:25–2:40 | The receipt on screen: fields shared, with whom, time, hash, signature. | "Last, a signed receipt with a time stamp: what was shared, with whom, and when. It is saved on your laptop. It is evidence. It is not a legal contract." |
| 8 | 2:40–3:00 | Numbers slide, then the closing line with the repo link. | "We measured it. Match accuracy: [ ] percent on 30 fake pairs. Profile quality: [ ] percent on 20 messy notes. Speed: [ ] seconds. Rahsia does not stop idea theft. It reduces early sharing and records what was shared. Rahsia: find the fit before you share the secret." |

**Spoken length:** about 330 words. At a calm pace that is about 2:30, which leaves room for the screen pauses.

## If something is not finished (cut rules)

| Missing | What to do |
|---|---|
| Draft generator | Delete the second half of scene 6. Say: "They see only the overlap." |
| Receipt | Delete scene 7. Add its seconds to scene 6. |
| Network monitor | In scene 5, show the "0 fields shared" screen only. Do not say "almost nothing was sent". |
| Match check only half-working | Record the profile builder (scene 4) for longer, and be honest: "This is the first step of Rahsia." |
| A number was not measured | Leave it out of scene 8. Never guess. |

## Recording tips

- Record the screen in 1080p. Zoom the browser to 125% so text is readable on a phone.
- Record voice in a quiet room. Do the voice after the screen, so you can match the timing.
- Do the live demo flow once as a dry run. Time each scene.
- Keep a backup screen recording of the full demo (TASKS.md asks for this too).
